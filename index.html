About Me

Behind
The
Lens

I'm an Interaction Design student at ArtCenter College of Design. My work spans UX, web development, and physical making, specifically focussing on projects driven by how design shapes the way people feel and move through the world. I'm working toward a future in automotive UX and technology, bringing that same human-centered sensitivity to the way people interact with vehicles.

Photography has been part of my life since childhood, a way of seeing that grew alongside me. As my love for cars deepened, the two passions naturally converged, and car photography became a serious hobby I keep returning to. This website is a compilation of the most memorable photos I’ve captured over the years.

The San Diego Automotive Museum, located in historic Balboa Park, explores the cultural and technological evolution of motorized vehicles.
